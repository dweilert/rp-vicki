The questions it helps answer

Most tools lead with feature names. RP is organized around the decisions a household actually has to make.

Can I retire earlier?

Try different retirement dates, income gaps, market crashes, inflation decades, and longer lifespans in the same plan.

How much can I spend?

When should we claim Social Security?

Five things to do before you decide

Useful whether or not you ever install RP.

  • Write down the date you want to stop working. Every other number in a plan hangs off it.
  • Add up guaranteed income. Social Security, pensions, annuities — what arrives whether markets cooperate or not.
  • Estimate real spending, not a budget. Twelve months of actual outflow beats a category worksheet.
  • Find the tax-rate gap. The years between retiring and RMDs are usually where Roth conversion room lives.
  • Check the survivor case. One filing status change can move the plan more than a market downturn.
